Suggest new features for Kimai on Kimai Roadmap

Many features in Kimai started as an idea from a user. If you’re missing something, post it on Kimai Roadmap.

Before you request a feature

Search Kimai Roadmap first; browsing works without an account. The most popular ideas are at the top (Trending), and you can also sort by Most wanted, Most discussed and Recent, or filter by activity, status and tags. If the idea already exists, follow it or vote for it instead of posting a duplicate.

Requesting a feature on Kimai Roadmap

Create an account

  1. Send an email to support@kimai.org asking for an invite to Kimai Roadmap
  2. We will send you an invitation email with a sign up link
  3. Follow the link and register
  4. That’s it! Now you can post, vote on, comment on and follow requests

Post a feature request

  1. Open Kimai Roadmap and go to All Feedback
  2. Click + next to “What would you like to have in Kimai?”
  3. Answer the three questions in the form: what you’re trying to do and what’s missing, in what situation it matters and why, and any examples or screenshots
  4. Check the similar posts shown below the form. If your idea is already on the list, vote for it instead
  5. Give your idea a title
  6. Click Submit your idea
  7. That’s it! Your request gets the status Open, and everyone can see it

You automatically follow your own requests and get notified when someone votes, comments, or the status changes.

Voting

You need to register in order to vote. You can vote on any request with the status Open, Planned or Started. Open the request and click Vote for this idea. Click again to remove your vote.

Commenting

You need to register in order to comment. You can comment on any request: write your comment in the Discussion section and click Post. Your comment is then visible to other users. To edit or delete a comment, click the ... icon.

Following

You need to register in order to follow a request. Open the request and click + Follow in the lower right corner. Click again to unfollow.

By default, notifications appear in the web interface. Click the bell icon in the upper right corner to view them.

To receive notifications by email, or only for status updates, click your profile icon and select My Settings. Scroll down to Notifications and choose the events you want to be notified about.

What happens after you submit

We review requests every week. Votes matter a lot, but they’re one signal among several: we also consider how many users a change helps, how much work it takes and how it fits Kimai’s direction. Each request shows its current status:

  • Open: new, waiting for votes and our first look
  • Planned: we intend to build it
  • Started: we’re working on it
  • Completed: it’s in Kimai
  • Declined: we’ve decided not to build it
  • Duplicate: we merged it with an existing request
