**[Asyntai](https://asyntai.com)** adds an AI chat assistant to your Kimai. It answers your users all day, in their language, from the instructions and the knowledge you give it.

## Features

- Chat assistant for signed-in users, on every Kimai page
- Chat assistant on the login page, for visitors
- A setting for each place, so you choose who sees the chat
- Connect your account with one button in **System → Asyntai AI Chatbot**
- Set up the instructions, the knowledge and the look in the Asyntai panel

An Asyntai account is necessary. You can create a free account from the plugin settings.
