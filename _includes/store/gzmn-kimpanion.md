Kimpanion connects your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to your existing Kimai server. Browse your timesheet, record your work, and manage timers without opening a browser.

## Features

- Browse time entries by week and day, with daily totals and running timers.
- Create, edit, and delete time entries with a customer, project, activity, and description.
- Start, stop, and restart timers, including timers started on another device connected to the same Kimai account.
- Choose light, dark, or system appearance.
- On Mac, keep your timer close in the menu bar and pin the app window while you work.

## Free access and optional subscriptions

Time entry and timer management are free for one saved Kimai account. An optional monthly or annual subscription unlocks:

- My Time and Team Time reports, with total hours, billable and non-billable breakdowns, and project and activity summaries.
- Multiple Kimai accounts and servers, with an account switcher.
- Optional merging of restarted timers into their original entries.

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. See the App Store for current regional pricing. Reports respect your Kimai account's permissions. Timer merging requires a server-side custom field and rounding configuration; the [setup guide](https://kimpanion.app/support/#troubleshooting) explains these requirements.

## Connect to Kimai

Install Kimpanion from the App Store, then enter your Kimai server's HTTPS URL and your API access token. You need an existing Kimai account with API access and permission to use the relevant customers, projects, and activities. A Kimai server or hosting plan is not included.

Your time entries remain on your Kimai server, and connection credentials are stored securely on your device. A connection to your server is required to load and save entries; offline editing and synchronization aren't supported.
