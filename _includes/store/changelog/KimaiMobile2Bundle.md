## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Offline-capable mobile web app for Kimai under `/mobile2/`
- Menu entry "Mobile app" in Kimai with QR code
- Day view of the last 4 weeks, start/stop timer, quick select, duration buttons
- Offline queue with automatic sync and conflict detection
- Billable: default value and optional checkbox (setting), respects Kimai permissions
- Kimai look and feel, light and dark mode
- Eight languages (en, de, fr, es, it, nl, pt, pl), date and time in the user's locale
- Subscription via Gumroad (monthly to yearly) with a free 7-day trial
