## 1.1.0

First public release of Lost Hours for Kimai.

- Your week as a timeline on the Kimai dashboard: work blocks, gaps and long gaps (red, threshold in the settings)
- Entries running in parallel are shown in parallel (lanes like a calendar), overlaps marked in red
- Optional: entry bars in the customer's color (setting)
- Click on a gap to log the missing time in Kimai's own dialog (begin and end prefilled)
- Tooltip with begin, end, duration and customer / project / activity
- Entries across midnight split, running timer counts until now
- Week paging, optionally together with Kimai's "Working hours" widget
- Kimai look and feel, light and dark mode
- Eight languages (en, de, fr, es, it, nl, pt, pl)
- Subscription via Gumroad (monthly or yearly) with a free 7-day trial
