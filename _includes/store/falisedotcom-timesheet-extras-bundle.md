Improvements to Kimai's record lists.

- **Collapsible days:** with Kimai's **Show daily stats in timesheet** preference on, each
  day's total row moves to the top of the day on **My times** and **All times**. Click it to
  collapse the day to just that row. One button collapses or expands all days, and the
  browser remembers which days are collapsed.
- **Closable filters:** on all of Kimai's lists, the filter menu next to the search field
  closes on a click outside it and gets a **Close** button.

### Permissions

The plugin adds no permissions and needs no configuration.

### Support

Report problems and ideas in the plugin's

### Works well with

This plugin is one of five made to work together; each also works on its own:

- [Quick start bar]({% link _store/{{ page.lang }}/falisedotcom-quick-timer-bar-bundle.md %}): start and stop time recording from one bar below the top bar of every page.
- [Reporting extras]({% link _store/{{ page.lang }}/falisedotcom-reporting-graphs-bundle.md %}): quarterly reports, charts on the user reports, a summary report and dashboard widgets.
- [Short time entries]({% link _store/{{ page.lang }}/falisedotcom-short-time-entries-bundle.md %}): type 945 for 9:45 or 10 for ten minutes in Kimai's time and duration fields.
- [Inline timesheet editing]({% link _store/{{ page.lang }}/falisedotcom-inline-timesheet-edit-bundle.md %}): edit your own records right in the record list.
