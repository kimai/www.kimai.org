Kimai is a registered European Union trademark (EUIPO No. 019012775) owned by Kevin Papst.

You are welcome to talk and write about Kimai and to refer to it. However, you may not use “Kimai” as the name of your own product, service or company. You must not give the impression that your offering is official or affiliated with Kimai or Kevin Papst.

We are happy when you talk about Kimai, build extensions and help spread the word about the project. This policy is meant to make that easier while preventing confusion with the official offerings.

Allowed without permission

  • Articles, books, courses, videos and talks about Kimai, including “Kimai” in the title, but not as the sole title.
  • Factual statements such as “compatible with Kimai”, “plugin for Kimai” or “consulting for Kimai”.
  • Redistributing unmodified Kimai under that name, for example as a Docker image, package or entry in an app catalog. Configuration and installed plugins do not count as modifications.

Names for plugins, integrations and apps

  • Recommended: a distinct name of your own, with “for Kimai” in the subtitle or description.
  • Allowed: your own product name followed by “for Kimai”, for example “JiraSync for Kimai”.
  • Not allowed: “Kimai” on its own or combined with a generic or platform term, for example “Kimai App”, “Kimai Mobile”, “Kimai Desktop”, “Kimai Docker”, “Kimai PWA”, “Kimai Client”, “Kimai Cloud”, “Kimai for Android” or “Kimai for iOS”. This applies regardless of spelling. These names are reserved for official products.
  • Other compound names containing “Kimai” (for example “KimaiTray”) require permission.
  • Standalone apps must state in their description that they are unofficial.

Hosting and SaaS

If you run Kimai for third parties (hosting, SaaS, managed service), you may not use “Kimai” in product, plan or company names, domains or advertisements. The logo and terms such as “official”, “original” or “partner” must not be used. You may describe hosting Kimai as a service on your own website in a factual way, together with the independence notice. Kimai Cloud is the only official offering.

Forks and modified versions

The Kimai source code is licensed under the AGPL, which does not include any trademark rights. Modified versions must use a name of their own and must not use the Kimai name or logo in the user interface or in advertising. The statement “based on Kimai” is permitted.

Domains, accounts and technical identifiers

  • “Kimai” must not be part of a domain name. Subdomains for your own installation (e.g. kimai.example.com) are allowed, but not for offerings to third parties.
  • Account, channel and organization names must not be “Kimai” or appear official.
  • Repository, package and image names may contain “kimai”, as long as the name of the organization or user does not cause confusion.
  • Confusingly similar names are assessed case by case.

Use of the logo

The logo may be used to refer to Kimai, for example in articles, talks or on the page of a plugin. It must not be used as an app icon, favicon, product or company logo or profile picture, nor in any modified form. The CC BY-SA 4.0 license covers copyright only and does not grant any trademark rights.

Advertising

In paid ads, “Kimai” must not appear in the headline, the text or the display URL. Bidding on “Kimai” as a keyword for competing offerings is not permitted.

Community

Online spaces (forums, mailing lists and the like) and in-person gatherings (meetups, user groups etc.) must not present themselves as officially representing Kimai.

Partners and merchandise

The designation “Kimai Partner” and the partner logo are reserved for listed partners. Physical products bearing the Kimai name or logo (e.g. stickers, t-shirts, mugs) require permission.

Permissions

We grant exceptions in writing (email is sufficient). They apply to one specific product, are non-transferable and can be revoked.

Independence notice

The following notice is only required for hosting and SaaS offerings, standalone apps, modified versions and products with an approved exception. It must be visible where the offering is described, for example on the product page, in the app store listing or in the README. It is not needed for articles, books, videos, consulting or plugins.

Kimai is a registered trademark of Kevin Papst. (Product name) is an independent project and is not affiliated with Kimai or Kevin Papst.

Violations and contact

If we notice a violation, we will first ask you to correct it. We reserve the right to remove listings from the Marketplace and to take legal action. Whether a product is listed in the Marketplace is decided independently of this policy.

When in doubt about your use of the Kimai name or logo, please contact Kevin Papst. Thank you for your understanding and support!
