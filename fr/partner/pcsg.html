About us

PCSG is an owner-managed IT and digital agency based in Solingen that has been a partner in the design, development, and long-term support of customized software solutions since 2005. Our goal is not only to implement digital tools from a technical standpoint, but also to integrate them into existing business processes in a way that creates measurable benefits in day-to-day operations. We provide our clients with comprehensive support—from the initial consultation and feasibility analysis through implementation and custom extensions to operation, maintenance, and support.

As an official Kimai partner, we assist companies with the implementation, configuration, and customization of Kimai. This includes custom plugins, interfaces to existing systems, technical consulting, error analysis, hosting and server services, as well as the long-term further development of the installation. We view standard solutions as a starting point: where specific processes or requirements exist, we develop tailor-made extensions.

Our work is based on personal collaboration, technical openness, and solution-oriented consulting. We foster a culture of open discussion, challenge existing approaches, and continuously expand our knowledge so that we can advise our clients on technologies that are both up-to-date and sustainable. Our collaboration is characterized by a passion for software development, transparent communication, and reliable support that extends beyond the completion of the project.

Since every company has different processes and requirements, the scope of services, timeline, and costs are calculated on a case-by-case basis in a transparent manner. Once the requirements have been clarified, clients receive a concrete, non-binding quote.
