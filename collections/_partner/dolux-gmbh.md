Dolux GmbH specializes in platform engineering, DevOps, and IT consulting. Founder Eugen Dorn, a certified technician in computer science, built his career in Linux, server, and storage environments and brings many years of experience designing and running on-premises infrastructure, container platforms such as OpenShift and Docker, and cloud environments on AWS and Azure.

As a Kimai partner, Dolux helps companies run Kimai reliably and securely, whether in their own data center, in the cloud, or in containers. From installation and configuration to automation, monitoring, and backups, all the way to updates and ongoing support, Dolux makes sure your time tracking runs smoothly and grows with your needs. Whether it's a short-term capacity gap, a specific project, or a long-term engagement, Dolux works in a personal, pragmatic way, as a true partner, to find the best solution together.
