Personal, clear and independent: IT that does what it should. With Klein IT Solutions, I support companies, freelancers and teams with all their IT needs from Bad Honnef, near Bonn. This includes workstations, networks and support, hosting, IT security, and custom software development. As a one-person business, I offer something that often gets lost with larger service providers: one fixed contact person who knows your setup and who you can reach directly.

I use Kimai myself every day. I set up Kimai in your own environment or host it for you on my own infrastructure, and I take care of updates, backups and monitoring. I also adapt Kimai to the way you work, from customers and projects to roles and permissions, and I train you and your team so that time tracking really becomes part of your daily routine.

Where Kimai can't do everything yet, I build it myself. For example, I created my own desktop client for Kimai: you can record your activities by voice, and they're automatically summarized as bullet points, optionally with AI that runs locally. I've also connected Kimai to my own CRM. So I don't only know Kimai from the user interface, but also from its API. If something is missing for you, I'll build it.

My goal is honest advice without technical jargon. I only recommend what you really need. I deliberately choose open software and digital independence, because your data belongs to you and you shouldn't depend on any provider, not even on me. Since every business works differently, I calculate the scope and costs individually and transparently. After a first conversation, you receive a concrete, non-binding quote. If you like, I can also take care of ongoing support for a fixed monthly price.
