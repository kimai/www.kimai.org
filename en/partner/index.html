Official Kimai Partner Program

Find the Kimai expert your project needs

Recognition is granted to organisations and developers who have demonstrated their competence with Kimai. Tell us about your expertise — we will connect you to the client waiting for your help.

Find a Partner Become a Partner
What it is

Recognition for the people who make Kimai work in the real world

The Partner Program identifies trusted experts around Kimai so clients can find help with confidence — and so the partners who have demonstrated their competence are properly acknowledged for it.

Service partners

Agencies, consultancies and integrators who install, host, support, train and tailor Kimai for organisations. Recognised for hands-on implementation experience and a track record of well-run deployments.

Development partners

Teams and individuals who build plugins, extensions and integrations on top of Kimai. Recognised for published, maintained work that extends the platform for the wider community.

HOW IT WORKS

A standard we maintain

Recognition follows a deliberate path. There is no instant sign-up — the designation is granted, held, and revisited over time.

1
Demonstrate competence

Show real implementation work, published extensions, or both — alongside a clear understanding of the platform.

2
Alignment review

We review the work and how the partner operates: company values, data protection and conduct towards customers.

3
Recognition & listing

Recognized partners receive the official designation, partner badge, client leads and a place in the partner directory.

4
Periodic review

Recognition is revisited on a yearly basis and reflects current standing.

Frequently asked questions

The term Official Kimai Partner is a designation awarded by Kimai to companies and developers who have demonstrated solid expertise with the platform and act in accordance with Kimai’s values. Partners operate independently; Kimai facilitates connections where needed but is not involved in project engagements that partners carry out for their clients.

The Partner Badge shows clients that a partner’s experience has been reviewed and recognised by Kimai. Recognition is valid for one year and is renewed following a review. For as long as recognition remains active, the partner may identify themselves as an Official Kimai Partner and display the Partner Badge on their own website, in marketing materials, and in client communications.

Companies and individuals who wish to become Official Kimai Partners must meet certain minimum requirements:

  • a credible professional presence
  • documented experience with Kimai
  • a respectful approach to open source and the community
  • up-to-date knowledge of supported Kimai versions
  • at least 3 client projects or 12 months of experience with Kimai
  • at least one named technical contact person
  • existing client references or case studies.”

As a first step, a company or individual wishing to become an Official Kimai Partner must submit a request as described on the Become a Partner page. If the minimum requirements are met, an online interview will be arranged. Following a successful interview and acceptance of the program terms, the applicant is recognised as an Official Kimai Partner.

This commission funds the operation and continued development of the Partner Program, the partner directory, and the plugin store through which partners are discovered and their services made visible.

There is currently no ranking system. Recognition as an Official Kimai Partner grants all partners the same benefits and obligations.
